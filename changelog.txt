Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-11-07
  Features:
    - Every locomotive charts the map around it like a radar.
    - Moving locomotives chart every scan interval; parked locomotives refresh on a slower timer.
    - Overlapping locomotives share chunks, so busy stations cost about the same as a single train.
    - Work is spread across ticks to avoid lag spikes on large networks.
    - Picks up locomotives from any mod, including ones created by script.
